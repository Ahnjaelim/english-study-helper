The Little Lighthouse

On a rocky island at the edge of the sea, there stood a little lighthouse. Every night, it sent its beam across the dark water, guiding ships safely home.

The lighthouse keeper was an old woman named Mabel. She had lived on the island for forty years, and she knew every wave and every wind by heart.

One stormy evening, the light flickered and went out. Mabel grabbed her lantern and climbed the narrow stairs, determined not to let a single ship be lost.
